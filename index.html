Your questions, answered for you.

What is HappyCall?

HappyCall is a reverse booking platform. You share the dates you can travel and the places you love, and accommodations that fit send you private, bookable offers instead of you searching through endless results.

Where does HappyCall operate?

We are starting in Brazil, with accommodations from beach inns and mountain chalets to farm hotels, colonial houses and city hotels. More regions will come as the network grows.

How do I receive offers?

Create a trip with your free dates and places, optionally name stays from your watchlist, and we match accommodations from our curated list. Invitations arrive in waves, usually once a week and more often when your dates are close.

Does HappyCall cost anything for travelers?

No. Creating trips, saving places and receiving offers is free. Partner accommodations pay only when a stay is booked.

How do I know a price is good?

Before an offer is sent we compare the rate with major booking sites, nearby dates and similar accommodations, so you can see whether it is unusually good and whether now is a good time to go.

What do accommodations see about me?

Accommodations see your dates, places and party size. Your contact details are shared only when you accept an offer.

Which accommodations are on HappyCall?

A curated list of independent places: inns, chalets, farm hotels, colonial houses, apartments and boutique hotels, each with editorial recommendations or guest feedback behind it.

Can I save places without choosing dates?

Yes. Your watchlist is private, dateless and commits you to nothing. We check those places first for any trip you create.

Can I cancel a booking?

Every offer shows its cancellation terms up front. They are set by each accommodation.

Is there a mobile app?

Today HappyCall works in your browser on any device. Mobile apps are on our wish list.

How can my accommodation join?

Independent accommodations can ask for access from the For accommodations page. You see where and when guests want to travel, build private offers and pay only when a stay is booked.
