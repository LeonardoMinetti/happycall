Privacy

This is a demonstration site. It has no accounts, no tracking and no backend: everything you type stays in your browser.

Your language choice, your trip request and your demo watchlist are kept in your browser's local storage so they survive a reload. You can clear them at any time from your browser settings.

A real HappyCall service would publish its full privacy policy here before launch.
