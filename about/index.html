Travel should start with your calendar

We all love to travel. Going somewhere new is the kind of experience that makes a year feel longer and richer.

Yet most travel websites are really just booking websites. Their job is to rank huge amounts of inventory against whatever you typed into a search box: rows of results, prices that seem to climb at checkout, and by the end, decision fatigue.

Booking sites are excellent at the last step of the journey, when you already know exactly what you want. We wanted something that lives upstream of that moment and carries you from the first spark of wanting to go, through discovery, to booking. And we wanted your time, your free weekends and your leftover vacation days, to sit at the centre of the marketplace.

That is why we are building HappyCall.

HappyCall makes it easy to take every trip on your list. You tell us when you are free and where you want to go, and we go looking for the best stays and rates to make it happen. We are starting in Brazil.

We do it in two ways:

  • We give accommodations a place to shop for you and send invitations directly.
  • We watch for rates that are rarely advertised in public.

Say you have two open weekends and you have been meaning to explore the coast or take the kids to the mountains. Tell HappyCall once, and a private invitation to a great place to stay can find you.

Hosts deserve a better platform too

Independent accommodations often hand a large share of every booking to the big online agencies, on top of placement fees and partner programs that make new guests more expensive each year.

Hospitality comes down to how you make someone feel. The people keeping person-to-person relationships alive deserve a tool that gives them more control over the rooms they have left.

For hosts, HappyCall is a reverse OTA: they are notified when eligible travelers list their trips, and they decide whether to send a private, discounted offer to fill unsold dates.

A little about rate parity

By listing on the big agencies, properties agree to rate parity, which limits how they can discount rooms in public. A private channel gives them more pricing flexibility, and our reverse model adds three advantages:

  • It is a private network. Only members receive booking invitations.
  • Our travelers really want to go. By listing where and when, they turn latent demand into active demand.
  • Everyone can win. When a host saves on distribution, there is more room for a better price for the traveler.

Let adventure find you

When there is an open weekend on your calendar, or your vacation days are piling up, or you simply need to get out there, try travel that starts with an invitation. Great places would love to host you.
